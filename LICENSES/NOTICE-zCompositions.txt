Python adaptation: nonrobust, nonclosed concentration inputs only;
explicit rejection of nonconvergence, invalid observations, closed row
totals, unidentifiable samples and replacement values at or above their
detection limits. This is a modified implementation, not the upstream R
package, and numerical equivalence has not yet been established.

Literature-based revision (2026-11-10): the Python EM backend uses Gaussian conditional regression (Monahan, 2006, doi:10.18637/jss.v016.i08), upper-truncated normal conditional first and second moments (Greene, Econometric Analysis, Chapter 19, Theorem 19.2), and maximum-likelihood EM updates normalized by N (Dempster, Laird and Rubin, 1977, doi:10.1111/j.2517-6161.1977.tb01600.x). It intentionally differs from upstream a44822d6 and is not a numerically equivalent port. The concentration output is an inverse-ALR conditional geometric point estimate, not an arithmetic conditional concentration mean. At most one censored component per sample is supported; ordinary missing components may coexist. Unsupported joint multiple-censoring problems are rejected, without fallback.
